# Screenshots

Screenshots of the viewer running the hosted demo (`streamlit_app.py`, synthetic comments only)
in Chromium, at 390x844 (phone, 2x) and 1280 wide (desktop, cropped to the page column), in
light and dark mode. `overlays/` shows the optional regional climate overlays and the regional
climate reference box; `at-a-glance/` shows the "At a glance" header.

The hardiness-zone screenshots show a layer simplified from the 2023 USDA Plant Hardiness Zone
Map GIS data (PRISM Climate Group, Oregon State University). This is not the official USDA Plant
Hardiness Zone Map — see planthardiness.ars.usda.gov. Each of them shows that disclaimer, and no
USDA-ARS or OSU logo appears (terms and citation:
`src/crop_explorer/data/overlays/NOTICE.md`).
