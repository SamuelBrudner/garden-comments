## Privacy policy

*Last updated: 20 October 2026*

Garden variety is a free, non-commercial website that shows gardeners' comments about crop
varieties and the gardens they were grown in. This policy explains what information the site
uses, where it comes from, and what happens to it. By using the site you agree to this policy
and to the [Terms of use](?page=terms).

### This site uses YouTube API Services

Some versions of this site show public comments from YouTube videos. Those comments are
obtained through **YouTube API Services**. YouTube is a Google service, so
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) applies to information YouTube
and Google hold. The public demo version of this site shows only made-up example comments,
and they are marked as made up.

### Information about you, the visitor

- **No accounts.** You never sign in, and the site never asks for your name, email address
  or YouTube account. It cannot see or change anything in your YouTube or Google account.
- **Your garden choices and searches** (region, hardiness zone, search words and so on) are
  kept only in the server's memory while your browser tab is open, so the page can show
  matching comments. They are not saved, not linked to you, and not written to the site's
  logs.
- **Logs** record only counts and technical events (for example, how many comments a search
  found). They never record your search words or any comment text.
- **Cookies and browser storage.** Streamlit, the software the site runs on, sets one
  technical cookie that protects the page against cross-site request forgery, and may keep
  display settings (such as light or dark mode) in your browser's local storage. The site
  sets no advertising or tracking cookies, shows no ads and does not use analytics. Usage
  statistics in Streamlit are turned off.
- **Hosting.** The public site is hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud, run by Snowflake Inc.
  Like any web host, it receives technical information such as your IP address and browser
  type when you visit. Snowflake handles that information under the
  [Streamlit privacy notice](https://streamlit.io/privacy-policy).
- **Links to other websites** open only when you click them. Those sites have their own
  privacy policies.

### Information about YouTube commenters

When a version of the site shows YouTube comments:

- **What is collected.** For each public comment and reply on the chosen video: the comment
  text, its YouTube comment id and the time it was posted, read with an API key through the
  YouTube Data API. **No author names, channel ids, profile pictures or like counts are
  collected or shown.**
- **How it is used.** Each comment is shown as written, with a link back to it on YouTube. An
  AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) reads each comment once, ahead of time, to note which crops
  and varieties it mentions and any growing conditions the commenter stated, such as a
  hardiness zone or "containers". Those notes are the site's own labels, not YouTube data, and
  each one points to the exact words it came from. They are used only to filter and group
  comments. Commenters are not profiled, scored or contacted.
- **Who it is shared with.** The comment text is shown to visitors of the site and was sent to
  the AI model for the step above. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.
- **How long it is kept.** At most 30 days. Before then the comments are fetched again from
  YouTube (a comment deleted or edited on YouTube is deleted or updated here too), or they are
  deleted.
- **Removal.** If you wrote a comment and want it removed from this site, delete or edit it on
  YouTube (it leaves this site at the next refresh, within 30 days), or ask for its removal as
  described below and it will be removed sooner.

### Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to remove a comment, go to the project's public
issue tracker:
[github.com/SamuelBrudner/garden-comments/issues](https://github.com/SamuelBrudner/garden-comments/issues).
To ask for a removal without posting the comment publicly, give only the video and the date
of the comment.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
